#Recommendations for Business Growth
​Double Down on Top Categories: Allocate a larger portion of advertising budget to Clothing and Accessories to scale top-line growth.

​Bundle Underperforming Items: Since Outerwear has lower sales, introduce cross-selling strategies—such as offering discounts on outerwear when bundled with high-demand clothing or footwear items.

​Maintain Omnichannel Checkout: Keep all payment methods active to prevent friction at checkout, as customers evenly utilize a variety of digital and traditional payment options.
